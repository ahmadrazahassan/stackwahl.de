# Screenshots and videos

Every screenshot and video on a profile, with its source. The content guard fails when a file or video id is missing here.

## Rules

- **Screenshots** come from vendor press material that the vendor offers for reporting (`origin: presse`), or later from the editors' own trial accounts (`origin: eigene`). Press images are labeled on the page as material of the vendor. They are converted to WebP, at most 1600 px wide, without other changes.
- **Videos** come only from a channel that the vendor links on its own website, or are embedded by the vendor itself. Title and channel were checked with the provider's oEmbed endpoint on the access date. They load only after a click (click-to-load, Section 3.6); before that no request goes to YouTube or Vimeo.
- No AI images, no screenshots from third-party reviews, no thumbnails downloaded from YouTube.
